Completing coordinates for the remainder — starting with the six projects of ₱150M or more — would allow the same check.

The November 13 revision resolved 30 of the flags raised on the October version, with no change to any amount. The open items above total ₱1.23 billion of the ₱17.6 billion program and can be addressed within the workbook before plenary.
