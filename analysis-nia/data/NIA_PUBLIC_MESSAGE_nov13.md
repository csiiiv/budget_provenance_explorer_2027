# PEOPLE'S BUDGET COALITION ANALYSIS OF NIA IRRIGATION PROJECTS
16 November 2026

Our People's Budget Coalition team has reviewed the latest version of the NIA FY 2027 irrigation annex—792 projects worth ₱17.6 billion in the NEP—as of 13 November 2026.

We confirm that NIA has addressed 43 distinct projects worth ₱1.97 billion . The corrections covered verifications of location and documentation of:

- 5 project title pairs we had flagged as possible duplicates (₱107.6 million combined) have been shown as distinct by NIA's own Senate-list coordinates and stationing.
- 3 region reassignments (₱900 million): Mamil, Catugan, and Santa Maria were moved from Region 13 to Region 2; Santa Maria was also renamed to Isabela.
- 30 further titles refined (₱966.4 million), 22 of which also received new or revised justification notes, bringing written reasons into rows that previously had none.

Every project amount and the ₱17.6 billion total stayed unchanged.

Beyond those items, 410 of the 792 Senate-list titles already carry Start and End coordinates, which allowed location checks against official boundaries. The remaining 382 projects (₱5.04 billion) do not yet have coordinates and cannot be checked the same way.

We will run further analyses once completed titles, programs of works, and other supporting documents are published before the Senate plenary. We will continue to work with the Senate and NIA to build a system of public verification so more people can check the completeness and correctness of the projects.

We laud the hard work of the National Irrigation Administration team, including the teams of the Senate Finance Committee and the Legislative Budget Research and Monitoring Office, for the openness to feedback and the professionalism they showed throughout this process. This is a model of constructive engagement on the budget process that we hope other executive agencies and the House of Representatives can follow.

CJ Sevilla, Lead Technical Analyst, BetterGovPH  
Kenneth Isaiah Ibasco Abante, Co-convenor, People's Budget Coalition
