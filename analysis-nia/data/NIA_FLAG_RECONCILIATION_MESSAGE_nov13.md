# People's Budget Coalition — Analysis of NIA Irrigation Projects

**FY 2027 National Expenditure Budget · National Irrigation Administration annex**
16 November 2026

Our People's Budget Coalition team reviewed the latest version of the NIA FY 2027 irrigation annex — 792 projects worth ₱17.6 billion in the NEP — as of 13 November 2026.

## About this statement

**Documents reviewed.** We compared two versions of the annex:

1. **October 10 version** — `NIA FY 2027 NEP OCTOBER 10 1043PM.xlsx`
2. **November 13 revision** — `NIA_NEP_2026-11-13.xlsx` (current)

All row numbers (r65, r480, …) refer to sheet rows of the November 13 file.

**How the workbook is organized.** Every project appears twice, with its location written in two places:

- **Budget schedule (columns B–G):** region (col E), province (col F), title (col G), NEP amount (col H). This is where the money is booked.
- **Senate submission list (columns P–V):** a separate list for the Senate with its own region/province grouping (cols T–U) and a longer title (col V). On 410 of the 792 projects, this title includes exact coordinates (e.g. `15°30'37.12"N, 120°56'26.19"E`).

The two lists are maintained separately and sometimes disagree. When they do, we check the evidence on each row — the coordinates and place names written in the titles themselves — against official PSGC boundaries and NIA's own division structure. The evidence sometimes backs the schedule and sometimes the Senate list (see Annex H).

**Terms.** RIS = River Irrigation System; CIS = Communal Irrigation System; SPIP/PIP = (Solar-Powered) Pump Irrigation Project; PCCP = Portland Cement Concrete Pavement; UPRIIS = Upper Pampanga River Integrated Irrigation Systems; PENRIS = Peñaranda River Irrigation System.

## Summary

**What NIA fixed in the November 13 revision.** NIA resolved 30 of our flags: 3 region corrections, 22 justification entries added or improved, and all 5 project pairs we suspected were duplicates were shown to be distinct, using NIA's own coordinates. Another 31 Senate-list titles were cleaned up. One other change — the re-booking of 5 UPRIIS rows to Tarlac — is listed under item 1 below.

**What still needs action: 26 rows worth ₱1.23 billion.**

1. **15 bookings that disagree with the workbook's own location data (₱235M).** In each case, the coordinates or the title indicate a different place than the booking. Ten rows have carried the same discrepancy since October: five pumps in Quezon, Isabela are booked under CAR even though Isabela is in Region II (Annex C); four pumps sit in BARMM provinces but are booked under Regions 10/12 — the province is right, the region is wrong (Annex C); and one row's own title says Nueva Ecija while the schedule says Bulacan (Annex D). The other five are the UPRIIS rows whose booking was changed to Tarlac in this revision while every other field still documents Nueva Ecija (Annex A).
2. **3 large moves made without a written justification (₱900M).** The moves themselves look correct, but the JUSTIFICATION column — used by 79 other rows — was left blank on all three: Mamil PIP (₱500M), Catugan PIP (₱200M), Santa Maria PIP (₱200M) (Annex G).
3. **1 possible double-listing (₱30M).** Two rows with the exact same title and the exact same ₱15M price, both in Quezon, Isabela, 14.8 km apart (Annex E).
4. **7 bookings that need a stated basis (₱80M).** Six PENRIS solar pumps sit 1–10 km outside Nueva Ecija, in Bulacan/Pampanga, and one pump sits 2 km into Cagayan. These may well be intentional — the ask is simply that the workbook say so (Annexes B, D).

Separately, **382 projects (₱5.04B) cannot be location-checked at all** because their titles carry no coordinates — including six projects of ₱150M or more (Annex F). This is a gap in coverage, not a count of defects: coordinates already exposed wrong bookings on 21 of the 410 checkable projects, so finishing the rest is where the next corrections will come from. Two small items complete the list: a province header misspelled as "Daval del Sur" (rows 156–159) and broken #REF! formulas (rows 1464–1465).

**Asks before plenary.** Resolve the 15 booking discrepancies — restoring or confirming each booking; confirm the San Juan pair; record justifications for all 8 moves; state the basis for the 7 cross-boundary bookings; and finish adding coordinates to the Senate-list titles, biggest projects first.

The same revision also made five re-assignments that went the wrong way (Annex A) and left all 8 moves without a written justification (Annex G). Those are the subject of the next section.

## What remains open

Those same coordinates also surfaced items we ask NIA to resolve before the Senate plenary. On 21 projects worth ₱312 million, NIA's own coordinates place the project in a different province or region than the schedule books it. They fall into two kinds. Ten have carried the same discrepancy since October — the booking disagrees with the coordinates, and no field changed in the revision: the Isabela-under-CAR five, the BARMM four, and the Peñaranda row that documents itself. The other five are the UPRIIS rows whose booking was moved to Tarlac in this revision while every other field still documents Nueva Ecija. In all fifteen the location evidence (coordinates, titles) is on one side and the booking on the other; the difference is that ten are uncorrected carry-overs and five result from an edit made in November. The remaining seven of the 21 are cross-boundary bookings — six PENRIS solar pumps 1–10 km into Bulacan/Pampanga and one pump 2 km into Cagayan. These may be deliberate, and the ask is only that the workbook say so, not that the booking move. A separate check of both lists across the whole schedule found one more conflict of a different kind: a row booked to Agusan del Norte but grouped under Surigao del Sur in the Senate list — there the booking is the correct side (Annex H).

The most clear-cut are the five UPRIIS service-road rows now booked under Tarlac. Nothing in either file disputes where they are: October booked all five under Nueva Ecija in both lists, and the November Senate list — untouched by the revision — still groups them under Nueva Ecija. Coordinates on four rows land 12 to 21 km inside Nueva Ecija, and the fifth row's title names Gen. Natividad, Nueva Ecija. The revision changed only the booking column, so the booking now contradicts every other field on the same rows. The resolution is mechanical either way: restore the Nueva Ecija booking, or, if Tarlac is intended, update the Senate-list fields to match (Annex A).

Two more gaps need attention. First, 382 projects (₱5.04B) still have no coordinates — a coverage gap rather than a defect, but an important one: checks on the 410 projects that do have coordinates caught booking discrepancies on 21 of them, so the remaining titles are where the next corrections will come from. One control defect also remains: the Establishment of Pump Irrigation Projects total rows (rows 1464–1465) now show #REF! errors in 18 cells (columns H–N and W) — two rows deleted in the November revision broke the formulas that add up this section. The October 10 file did not have this defect. Second, none of the 8 booking moves carries a written justification, even though the workbook's JUSTIFICATION column is actively used — 79 other rows have justification text — so the blank cells on exactly the 8 moved rows stand out. Three of the unexplained moves are large: Mamil PIP (₱500M), Catugan PIP (₱200M), and Santa Maria PIP (₱200M) — ₱900M moved between regions with no recorded justification.

We will run further analyses once NIA completes the Senate-list titles and re-verification comes in before plenary — especially on costing and on whether bookings match site coordinates. We will keep working with the Senate and NIA toward public verification, so more eyes can improve the budget as documents are uploaded.
